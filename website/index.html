WHY SMSecure

Messaging built around privacy and transparency

SMSecure keeps its core functionality understandable, inspectable, and under the user’s control.

Open source

The complete source code is publicly available for review, auditing, and contribution.

Encrypted conversations

Compatible SMSecure users can verify identities and communicate using encrypted messages.

No advertising or tracking

SMSecure contains no advertising SDKs and does not use analytics services to track users.

Local-first messaging

Messages and contacts remain on the device and are not uploaded to an SMSecure application server.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Only optional diagnostics leave the device

Diagnostic logs are submitted only when the user explicitly chooses to send them. They are sanitized before upload, encrypted in transit, and automatically deleted within 14 days.
